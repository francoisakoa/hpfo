normal Ziggurat, bounded integers and choice algorithms. The normal tables
are copied unchanged; the algorithms are adapted to the PF C++ API.

## METIS (optional, externally linked)

PF 1.3.1 can call the METIS 4 or METIS 5 NodeND interface for structural
ordering. METIS headers and binaries are not bundled. Obtain them from the
chosen distribution and retain its license. Validation used Coin-METIS 4
provided with CasADi, and METIS 5.1.0 sources distributed with PyMetis 2025.2.2
for the portable LDL tests. Eigen supplies both dense LDLT (auto) and sparse
SimplicialLDLT<double> (explicit METIS). No SuiteSparse LDL code is bundled.
