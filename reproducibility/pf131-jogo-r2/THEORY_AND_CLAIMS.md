# Scope of theoretical and experimental claims

The practical capped, adaptive and archive-dependent HPF implementation is not
covered by a convergence guarantee for an idealized uncapped surrogate.
The implementation does not explicitly impose the theoretical delta separation.

Full support must be stated for nonempty relatively open sets/neighborhoods,
not positive probability of reaching a prescribed individual continuous state.
Topological full support must not be conflated with every stronger measurable
irreducibility property without the additional assumptions required.

The completed component study concerns LJ75 and LJ102 only.
It measures removals from an integrated algorithm, including grouped removals,
not the standalone quality of every mechanism.
Distinguish target-attainment effects from evaluation/time effects.

Baseline conclusions concern the specific implementations, parameterizations,
initializations, local solvers and adopted comparable-budget protocol.
They do not establish superiority over entire algorithmic families.
Applicability beyond Lennard-Jones clusters is not empirically established here.
